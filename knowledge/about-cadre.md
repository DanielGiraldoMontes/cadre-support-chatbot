# About Cadre AI

## Summary

Cadre AI is an AI strategy and implementation consultancy. The firm's positioning is "from AI confusion to AI confidence": most companies know they should be using AI but don't have a clear plan, and Cadre acts as an embedded team that finds the highest-value opportunities, implements them, and trains the client's people so the change actually sticks.

## Key facts

- Founded in San Diego, California, in November 2024.
- Works with businesses ranging from startups to large enterprises, with particular depth in lower middle market private equity–backed companies, professional services firms, and financial services organizations.
- Official service partner of both OpenAI and Anthropic; also works with Google, Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce, and Snowflake, and can access a broad range of models through OpenRouter.
- Approach: go department by department inside a client's business, identify the highest-ROI AI opportunities, implement the tools/workflows/agents, then train the team so adoption sticks — rather than handing over a slide deck and walking away.
- Core belief: companies that treat AI as a scattered set of individual tools (some employees on ChatGPT, others on Claude, no shared strategy) create real risk — wasted spend, inconsistent results, and sensitive data handled inconsistently. Cadre's role is to bring structure and ownership to that.
- The team includes AI strategists, AI managers, and AI engineers working from a shared framework across engagements.
