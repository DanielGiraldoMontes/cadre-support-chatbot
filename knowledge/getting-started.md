# Getting Started with Cadre AI

## Summary

The verified, official next step for anyone who wants to start working with Cadre AI is to talk to an AI strategist — this is Cadre's consistent call-to-action across their site.

## Key facts

- The verified path is Cadre's contact page — framed as "Talk to an AI Strategist." This project does not have a separate, verified self-serve booking/calendar link (e.g. a direct Calendly URL) — do not invent one.
- Getting the AI Maturity Index scored is also positioned as an entry point — see `ai-maturity-index.md`.
- There is no verified separate "sign up" or account-creation flow distinct from initiating contact — engagement starts with a conversation, not a self-serve signup.

## How the chatbot should handle this

- If asked "how do I get started" or "how do I book a call" → tell them the verified next step is reaching out to talk with an AI strategist, and offer the escalation/contact path this project has actually implemented (see `PLAN.md` Phase 9, Booking Flow).
- Do not invent a specific calendar tool, meeting length, or availability — only state that a conversation with a strategist is the next step.

## If asked something not covered here

Specifics about what happens in a first call, typical engagement timelines, or onboarding steps beyond "talk to a strategist" are not public — escalate rather than guessing.
