# Industries Cadre AI Works With

## Summary

Cadre AI works with companies of many sizes and sectors, but is especially valuable for businesses with manual, people-heavy workflows that get less efficient as they scale — which is why B2B and B2C services companies, and private equity–backed companies looking to grow without ballooning headcount, are a particularly strong fit.

## Industries served (the finite, canonical list)

- Professional Services
- Private Equity
- Real Estate
- Financial Services
- Mortgage & Lending
- Construction
- Retail & E-commerce
- Manufacturing & Logistics
- Hospitality

This list is exhaustive for topic-matching purposes — if a user names an industry from this list, answer confidently that Cadre works in that space. If they name something adjacent but not on the list (e.g. "healthcare," "insurance"), do not claim a dedicated track exists; say Cadre works across a broad range of industries with the above as its named focus areas, and offer to connect them with the team to confirm fit for their specific case.

## Private equity note

Cadre has specific messaging for PE-backed companies: helping with deal sourcing, due diligence acceleration (CIM analysis, financial modeling, contract review), relationship intelligence, and real-time portfolio monitoring — framed around expanding EBITDA without proportionally growing headcount.
