# Cadre AI's Approach to LLM Selection

## Summary

Cadre AI does not push a single "house" model on clients. Part of their AI Strategy service is specifically helping a business select and configure the LLM(s) that best align with their existing tech stack and business goals.

## Key facts

- Cadre works across the major model providers — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Microsoft — plus a wider range of models accessible through OpenRouter, rather than being locked into one vendor.
- Their framing for why this matters: many companies already have AI happening inside them in an ungoverned way — some employees using ChatGPT, others using Claude, no shared strategy, and often sensitive company information being pasted into tools without oversight. That fragmentation is treated as a real business risk, not just an inefficiency.
- The fix Cadre positions itself as offering: bringing that fragmented, ad hoc usage into a centralized, secure, and intentionally chosen setup — the right model(s) for the right use case, governed rather than accidental.
- Model choice is treated as a means to a business outcome (fit with existing tools, cost, data handling requirements), not a technology preference for its own sake.

## If asked something not covered here

Specific technical criteria Cadre uses to select a model for a given client, or named client examples, are not public — escalate rather than inventing evaluation criteria.
