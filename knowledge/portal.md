# Cadre Client Portal

## Summary

Cadre AI offers clients a centralized portal to track the tools, agents, training, and results tied to their engagement — giving them one place to stay aligned on what's been implemented and what it's delivering.

## Key facts

- Purpose: track tools, agents, training progress, and measurable results in one place, so both the client and Cadre stay accountable to the plan.
- This project does not have a verified, public portal login URL. Do not invent one.

## How the chatbot should handle this

- If asked "what is the client portal" or "what can I track in it" → answer using the summary above.
- If asked "how do I log in" or "what's the portal URL" or any account-specific access question → the chatbot has no verified login URL and no ability to authenticate anyone. Say plainly that it doesn't have access to portal accounts, and escalate — direct them to the Cadre team for account-specific help.
- Never imply the chatbot itself can check, look up, or modify anything inside a client's actual portal account.

## If asked something not covered here

Portal features beyond what's listed above, or anything account-specific, are not verified — escalate rather than guessing.
