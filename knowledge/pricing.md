# Cadre AI Pricing

## Summary

Cadre AI does not publish pricing. This is expected for a bespoke strategy/implementation consultancy — engagement scope varies enough by client that a public price list wouldn't be meaningful.

## How the chatbot should handle this

- Never state, estimate, or imply a price, price range, or pricing model (e.g. "typically costs around X," "starts at Y") — none of that is verified, and inventing a number here is one of the highest-risk hallucinations this bot can make.
- The correct response to any pricing question is to say plainly that pricing depends on scope and isn't published, and to route them to talk with an AI strategist to get a real answer for their situation (see `getting-started.md`).
- This applies even to indirect pricing questions ("is this expensive," "what's a typical budget," "do you have a starter package") — same answer, same escalation.

## If asked something not covered here

There is nothing else to add here by design — any pricing specifics should always route to escalation, not to this file.
