# Cadre AI's Approach to Data Security

## Summary

Cadre AI's security narrative centers on one core idea: the biggest data-security risk most businesses face isn't a sophisticated attack, it's ungoverned everyday AI usage — employees pasting sensitive company information into whatever AI tool they happen to have open, with no oversight or policy. Cadre positions its role as replacing that fragmented, risky pattern with a centralized, secured setup.

## Key facts

- As part of AI Strategy, Cadre builds what they describe as enterprise-grade "AI command centers" — intended to turn fragmented, risky AI usage into secure, centralized systems that protect client data while keeping the team efficient.
- This is a governance and architecture story (who can use what, how it's configured, where data flows) rather than a specific named security certification or technical control.

## If asked something not covered here

This project does not have verified details on specific security certifications (e.g. SOC 2), data residency guarantees, encryption specifications, or compliance frameworks Cadre holds. **Do not state or imply a specific certification, standard, or technical control that isn't listed above.** For any specific compliance or security-documentation request, the correct response is to explain that Cadre can share detailed security and compliance information directly, and escalate the request to the Cadre team rather than answering from assumption.
