# Cadre AI Services

## Summary

Cadre AI offers four core services. They're often delivered together as part of one engagement, not purchased individually.

## The four services

**AI Strategy**
Helps a business figure out where AI actually creates value for them specifically — not a generic tool list, but a prioritized plan based on the business's own processes, tied to measurable outcomes like revenue growth and margin improvement.

**AI Leadership & Facilitation**
Focused on people, not tools. Training and change-management work that moves executives and teams from hesitant/skeptical about AI to confident, effective daily users — grounded in behavioral science and real implementation experience, not just a one-off workshop.

**AI Engineering**
Hands-on building: combining existing tools, workflow automation, and custom AI agents to solve specific business problems where off-the-shelf tools fall short.

**AI Agents**
Autonomous systems built to handle complex, multi-step tasks on their own, rather than just assisting a human step by step.

## Departments Cadre works within

Beyond industry vertical, Cadre also organizes engagements by business function/department: Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Executive Leadership, Finance, Operations, Technology, and Legal.

## Philosophy on tooling

Cadre doesn't default to building custom software. If a tool the client already uses has AI features available, Cadre helps them activate and adopt those first. When an off-the-shelf tool is the better fit, they recommend it. Custom-built solutions are reserved for cases where no faster or better option exists.
